# Job Description: Associate – Strategy & Business Operations

**Company:** NovaWell Consumer Brands  
**Department:** Strategy & Business Operations  
**Location:** Gurugram, Haryana, India  
**Experience:** 3–6 years  
**Employment Type:** Full-time  
**Reporting to:** Head of Strategy & Business Operations

## About the Role

We are looking for a data-driven and execution-oriented professional to join our Strategy & Business Operations team. The role involves working closely with leadership to identify growth opportunities, improve operational efficiency, automate business processes, and drive cross-functional strategic initiatives.

The ideal candidate combines analytical problem-solving, business judgment, stakeholder management, and a hands-on approach to execution.

## Key Responsibilities

**1. Strategy & Business Planning**
- Support leadership in developing and executing strategic initiatives aligned with business objectives.
- Conduct market research, competitor benchmarking, and business analysis to identify growth opportunities.
- Build business cases, evaluate strategic options, and provide data-driven recommendations.
- Track strategic projects and communicate progress, risks, and outcomes to senior stakeholders.

**2. Operations Excellence**
- Identify process inefficiencies and implement improvements across procurement, supply chain, inventory, and business operations.
- Develop standard operating procedures (SOPs) and establish performance measurement frameworks.
- Coordinate with cross-functional teams to improve execution speed, reduce manual work, and enhance operational efficiency.
- Monitor operational bottlenecks and implement scalable processes as the business grows.

**3. Data Analytics & Business Intelligence**
- Build and maintain dashboards using Excel and Power BI to monitor business performance.
- Analyse sales trends, SKU performance, inventory movement, procurement costs, and channel-level profitability.
- Define and track key performance indicators (KPIs) to support business reviews and decision-making.
- Translate complex datasets into actionable insights for leadership.

**4. Automation & Digital Transformation**
- Identify repetitive workflows suitable for automation using AI tools, APIs, low-code platforms, or scripting.
- Collaborate with technology and business teams to implement workflow automation.
- Evaluate automation outcomes using time saved, error reduction, adoption, and process efficiency.
- Document automated workflows and support their adoption across departments.

**5. Cross-Functional Program Management**
- Drive projects involving Sales, Marketing, Finance, Procurement, Supply Chain, and Technology.
- Define project milestones, owners, dependencies, and success metrics.
- Manage multiple priorities, resolve execution bottlenecks, and ensure timely delivery.
- Prepare management updates, presentations, and business review materials.

## Required Qualifications and Skills

- Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Business, Economics, or a related field.
- 3–6 years of experience in strategy, business operations, program management, consulting, or a fast-growing business.
- Advanced Excel skills, including data analysis, lookup functions, pivot tables, and financial analysis.
- Experience building dashboards and interpreting business performance metrics.
- Strong analytical thinking, structured problem-solving, and attention to detail.
- Demonstrated ability to manage cross-functional projects and multiple priorities.
- Excellent written communication, stakeholder management, and presentation skills.
- Ability to turn ambiguous business problems into structured execution plans.

## Preferred Qualifications

- MBA from a recognised institution.
- Experience in D2C, FMCG, consumer brands, e-commerce, or retail.
- Working knowledge of Power BI, SQL, and business intelligence tools.
- Experience with AI-assisted workflows, API integrations, or process automation.
- Exposure to procurement analytics, inventory planning, pricing, or unit economics.

## Success Metrics: First Six Months

- Deliver dashboards that improve visibility into business and operational performance.
- Identify and implement measurable process-efficiency improvements.
- Improve the reliability and timeliness of management reporting.
- Successfully execute at least two cross-functional strategic initiatives.
- Identify automation opportunities and measure their business impact.
- Provide actionable recommendations supported by data and business analysis.

## What We Value

We value ownership, structured thinking, execution discipline, curiosity, and the ability to work effectively in a fast-paced environment. Candidates should be comfortable moving between strategic problem-solving and hands-on operational execution.
