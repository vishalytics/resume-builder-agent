Operations Strategy Associate
Company: Northstar Consumer Products

Role overview: Work with senior leaders to improve business operations, translate strategic priorities into measurable programs, and support growth initiatives across a consumer products business.

Responsibilities:
- Build operating dashboards and track business KPIs for leadership.
- Coordinate cross-functional projects with operations, sales, finance, supply chain, and product teams.
- Identify manual processes and recommend workflow automation and process improvements.
- Conduct market and competitor research to support pricing and go-to-market decisions.
- Prepare concise progress updates and communicate risks, decisions, and outcomes to stakeholders.

Required qualifications:
- Experience in business operations, strategy, program management, or a founder's office.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Demonstrated project coordination and stakeholder communication.

Preferred:
- Experience with Excel, Power BI, or SQL.
- Consumer products or direct-to-consumer experience.
